1. SALES-ORDER PREREQUISITES

A sales order may be created only when:
- Permanent customer_id is available
- Lead and thread_id are available
- A valid quotation version exists
- PO validation has succeeded
- Commercial and operational revalidation has succeeded
- Required approvals are valid
- Inventory has been reserved or production has accepted the requirement

2. ORDER WON DEFINITION

ORDER_WON means the business has received valid contractual acceptance and
all internal acceptance conditions have passed.

Commercial interest alone does not mean ORDER_WON.

3. HANDOFF PACKAGES

Finance receives:
- Customer and credit information
- PO and quotation references
- Invoice value and payment terms

Production receives:
- Product specifications
- Quantity
- Required completion date
- Capacity allocation

Dispatch receives:
- Delivery address
- Transport requirements
- Dispatch deadline

Sales receives:
- Customer communication history
- Commitments and approval record

4. CONFIRMATION

A department is marked notified only after provider confirmation.
A failed handoff creates an external-system failure and must be retried.

5. AUDIT

The final audit must record:
- customer_id
- lead_id
- thread_id
- quotation_id
- PO ID
- sales_order_id
- handoff_id
- policy versions
- approval IDs
- departments notified
