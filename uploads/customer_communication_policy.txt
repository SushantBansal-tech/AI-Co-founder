1. APPROVED CHANNELS

Approved channels are email, WhatsApp and website responses.
Customer replies must be correlated with the existing lead thread_id.

2. AUTOMATIC MESSAGES

Jarvis may automatically send:
- Inquiry acknowledgements
- Requests for missing inquiry information
- Routine quotation reminders
- Delivery-status notifications based on confirmed data

3. APPROVAL-REQUIRED MESSAGES

Approval is required for:
- Negotiated price commitments
- Credit-term commitments
- Complaint settlements
- Legal or contractual statements
- Compensation offers
- Cancellation of confirmed orders

4. FOLLOW-UP FREQUENCY

First reminder: 3 days after quotation.
Second reminder: 7 days after quotation.
Final reminder: 14 days after quotation.
Stop reminders when the customer replies or opts out.

5. MESSAGE RULES

Messages must be professional and factual.
Jarvis must not invent stock, pricing, delivery or credit information.
Messages containing prices must use validated PostgreSQL results.
Provider delivery confirmation must be recorded before marking a message sent.

6. PRIVACY

Do not expose internal costs, minimum margins, authority limits or other
customers' information.
