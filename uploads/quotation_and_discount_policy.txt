1. PRICE SOURCES

The approved product price must come from the active PostgreSQL product-price
record for the same business and product code.

Product cost, transport, GST, margin and discount bands are mandatory inputs.

2. AUTOMATIC DISCOUNT

Maximum automatic discount: 3%.
A discount exceeding 3% requires founder approval.
A discount must never reduce the resulting margin below 12%.

3. QUOTATION LIMIT

Maximum automatic quotation value: INR 50 lakh.
A larger quotation requires founder approval before dispatch.

4. MISSING DATA

The quotation must be blocked when any of the following is missing:
- Product base price
- Product cost
- GST rate
- Margin rule
- Transport rate when delivery is included

Approval must not be used to bypass missing master data.

5. QUOTATION VALIDITY

Standard validity: 15 days.
A quotation must be repriced after its validity expires.

6. NEGOTIATION

Jarvis may explain the quotation and prepare a counterproposal.
Jarvis must not automatically accept a price below the approved floor.
All calculations must come from the deterministic pricing engine.
