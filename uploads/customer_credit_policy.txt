1. CUSTOMER CLASSIFICATION

New customer:
- Advance payment is preferred.
- Any credit terms require founder approval.

Existing good-standing customer:
- Standard credit may be considered using payment history and exposure.

Overdue customer:
- New credit is blocked until finance review.

2. REQUIRED CREDIT INPUTS

Credit decisions require:
- Customer identity
- Current credit limit
- Outstanding invoice amount
- Overdue amount
- Previous payment behaviour
- Proposed order value
- Requested payment terms

3. AUTOMATIC RESTRICTIONS

Jarvis cannot:
- Increase customer credit limits
- Extend payment days
- Ignore overdue invoices
- Override a finance block
- Approve incomplete credit data

4. APPROVAL

All changes to customer credit require founder or finance-manager approval.
Approval must record the approver, reason, expiry and policy version.

5. MISSING DATA

Missing exposure, payment history or credit-limit information results in
REQUIRE_MORE_INFORMATION or BLOCKED_MASTER_DATA.
