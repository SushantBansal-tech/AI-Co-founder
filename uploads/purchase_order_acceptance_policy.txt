1. REQUIRED PO FIELDS

A PO must contain:
- PO number
- PO date
- Buyer company
- Buyer GSTIN where applicable
- Product code or unambiguous product specification
- Quantity and unit
- Unit rate
- GST rate
- Total amount
- Delivery destination
- Delivery date or schedule
- Payment terms
- Reference quotation number

2. VALIDATION

The PO must be compared with the latest approved quotation version.

3. CRITICAL MISMATCHES

The following require correction or approval:
- Different product grade
- Different product code
- Price below approved quotation
- Incompatible payment terms
- Incorrect GST
- Unsupported delivery commitment
- Quantity outside the approved tolerance

4. REVALIDATION

Before order acceptance, recheck:
- Quotation validity
- Current product price
- Inventory availability
- Production capacity
- Delivery commitment
- Customer credit exposure
- Approval expiry
- Active policy version

5. OUTCOMES

Exact match: continue to order revalidation.
Minor mismatch: request approval.
Material mismatch: request a corrected PO or revised quotation.
Missing mandatory information: request more information.

6. ORDER STATUS

Receiving a positive email is not automatically ORDER_WON.
The normal sequence is:
CUSTOMER_ACCEPTED_COMMERCIALS
AWAITING_PO
PO_RECEIVED
PO_VALIDATED
ORDER_WON
